# Colonies and Star Systems

Human-machine civilization spans a roughly 30 light-year radius from Sol.
Most bodies are airless rocks. Colony worlds are few, mostly empty, with
a handful of cities and large natural environments.

## Sol System

The origin. Still the most populated and developed system. Earth,
Mars, the Belt, the outer moons.

## Interstellar Routes

Not all routes are equal. ISM density varies — denser regions mean
faster stardrive acceleration but more shield ablation. The Local Bubble
near Sol is thin (~0.1 atoms/cm³), making nearby departures sluggish.
Denser regions near spiral arms offer 5-50 atoms/cm³.

Route knowledge is valuable. The best paths between systems — balancing
ISM density, distance, and hazards — are closely guarded trade secrets.
