# Colonies and Star Systems

Human-machine civilization spans a roughly 20 light-year radius from Sol,
about seventy star systems.
Most bodies are airless rocks. Colony worlds are few, mostly empty, with
a handful of cities and large natural environments.

## Sol System

The origin. Still the most populated and developed system. Earth,
Mars, the Belt, the outer moons.

## Interstellar Routes

Not all routes are equal. The entire 20 ly inhabited sphere sits inside
the Local Bubble (~0.1 atoms/cm³). Spiral-arm densities are not on this
map. What varies is local clouds and filaments — a factor of a few, not
three orders of magnitude. Nothing aboard collects the medium, so density
does not touch speed or fuel. Denser gas means more grains for the FEL.

Route knowledge is valuable. The best paths between systems — balancing
density, distance, and grains — are closely guarded trade secrets.

Typical crossings for a courier, which burns, coasts, and brakes: α Cen
~93 years; a 10 ly hop ~151 years; 20 ly ~231, on one tank. Most routes
are chains of hops between IS depots. A system without a depot is a
slow-boat destination: a courier carries its way home, and the round
trip runs a quarter longer. See [Ship
Systems](ship-systems.md#the-crossing).
