# Setting

Space opera, 600 years in the future. Physics remains stuck at 21st century
understanding, but engineering has advanced fantastically.

## Technology

- **No FTL.** The light barrier is never broken by humans, augmented humans,
  or AIs.
- **Stardrives**: Interstellar Systems sells a black box, and the
  only fuel it burns: metastable metallic hydrogen shot nobody else
  can make — spacers call it dust. Dust in, fusion torch out. There is
  no speed wall, only the rocket equation: a full hold cruises at
  **~0.07c**, and an empty one reaches **0.10c**. Every refuel is an IS transaction. They sell drives rarely, to buyers they choose.
  See [Factions](factions.md) and [Ship Systems](ship-systems.md).
- **Nanofabrication**: Manufacturing any object from raw materials is trivial
  given a design. This creates post-scarcity for most goods.
- **Black-boxed tech**: Some items (powerful weapons, stardrives, etc.) have
  closely guarded internals with multiple mechanisms to prevent reverse
  engineering. This preserves economic scarcity where it matters.
- **Human form**: Humans mostly cling to a single physical form, augmenting and
  repairing it rather than shedding it entirely.
- **Implants**: Neural and physical augmentation is widespread. Ship systems can
  be operated through implants as well as physical consoles.
- **AI**: AIs above a statutory complexity threshold are legal persons with
  full rights — a status won in the AI Crisis of 2035-2039 and codified in the
  AI Compact. Sub-threshold AIs are tools. The line between the two is
  philosophically uncomfortable and never fully resolved. See [History](history.md)
  for the Compact's provisions and evolution.

## Civilization

- Human-machine civilization has spread within a roughly 30 light-year radius
  from Sol, expanding slowly: stardrives are rare, and a typical hop is a
  century. The sphere sits entirely inside the Local Bubble.
- Most planets, moons, and bodies are airless rocks. Colony worlds exist but are
  relatively few, mostly empty with a few cities and large natural environments.
- The aesthetic is far-future cyberpunk — advanced enough to feel alien, but
  grounded enough that players can relate.

## Economics

Nanofabrication makes most manufactured goods trivially cheap. Scarcity
exists only where it's enforced or fundamental: black-boxed designs that
can't be copied, raw elemental feedstock that must be mined or imported,
passage for persons who must physically travel, and unique objects that
can't be replicated.

Between star systems, trade operates through a futures market over laser
links, settled in local currencies or commodity quasi-currencies (³He,
platinum). There is no universal currency. Reputation is the
only enforcement mechanism.

See [Trade](trade.md) for full details — commodities, the futures market,
settlement, and the role of Interstellar Systems in shaping trade routes.

## Governance

There is no central government. The AI Compact serves as a shared
constitutional framework, adopted independently by each polity. Enforcement
is through trade networks and social pressure, not a central authority.
See [History](history.md) for how this evolved.

With no FTL communication, each colony is effectively sovereign. Public
news and contract data travel at lightspeed over the laser net; people,
bulk archives, and the version of events anyone trusts arrive with the
next hull, a century late. Political alliances form and dissolve over
the generations between visits.
